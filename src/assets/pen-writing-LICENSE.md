The creator describes a studio recording of a pen writing on paper. The public high-quality preview was used as source:
https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/234/234016_3735801-hq.mp3

`pen-writing.mp3` is a 0.85-second excerpt starting at 3 seconds, converted to mono with gentle fades, bandwidth filtering and level adjustment for short interface feedback. The full source recording is not included in the site.
